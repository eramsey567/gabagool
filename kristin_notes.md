# Puzzle & Journal Books: Working Notes

**Goal:** puzzle books (word search, sudoku, crossword, 3-in-1) and journals for Amazon KDP that are clearly better than the average listing. Every choice below is meant to be specific enough to act on.

> Numbers marked *(verify)* come from KDP's published rules as of 2026. Check them in KDP's royalty calculator and help pages before each launch.

---

## 1. What "above the slop" means

Most low-effort Amazon puzzle books fail in the same ways. Each item here is a rule we follow.

| Common slop problem | Our rule |
|---|---|
| Tiny print, cramped grids | Large print really is large: grid letters and digits **18pt minimum** in any book labeled "large print" |
| Answer-key errors | Every puzzle checked by software before printing. **Zero errors is the standard** |
| Sudoku with more than one solution, or wrong difficulty labels | Every sudoku has **exactly one solution**. Difficulty is graded by the solving techniques needed, not just the number of given digits |
| Random or off-theme word lists | Each puzzle has a sub-theme, and every word fits it. No filler words, and no repeated lists |
| Puzzles lost in the spine | Wide inside (gutter) margin so puzzles never curve into the binding (see Section 4) |
| Covers that promise more than the inside delivers | Cover and "Look Inside" images show real interior pages |
| Hard-to-find solutions | Answer key in the back, sorted by puzzle number, with the page number of each puzzle |

**Final check before publishing:** order a printed proof copy and try a few puzzles with a pen, first flat on a table, then held in one hand.

## 2. Book sizes

Choose the size first, because it determines everything else.

| Use | KDP trim size | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **At home** | 8.5 × 11 in | Large print word search, sudoku, 3-in-1 | Room for big grids and 1 puzzle per page. Most top-selling large print books use this size |
| **Handheld / to go** | 6 × 9 in | Travel puzzle books, journals | Fits a purse or carry-on. Too small for large print word search; smaller grids (13 × 13) work |
| **Small journal** | 5.5 × 8.5 in | Journals, pocket sudoku | Common gift size for journals |

- **Paperback only** to start, on **white paper for puzzles** (sharpest contrast) and **cream paper for journals** (warmer, better to write on).
- Plan a **handheld version and an at-home version** of the best sellers, not of every book.

## 3. Product lines

### 3-in-1 books (word search + crossword + sudoku)
- **Price:** $12.99–14.99
- **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in, about **150–200 pages**
- **Mix:** about 1/3 of each puzzle type, alternating so the reader doesn't get 60 sudokus in a row
- **Difficulty:** increases through the book. Label each section Easy / Medium / Hard.

### Single-theme word search
- **Price:** $9.99–12.99
- **Size:** 8.5 × 11 in, 100 puzzles, 1 per page
- **Grid:** 15 × 15, letters 20–22pt, word list 16pt+ in 2–3 columns under the grid
- **Extras:** a short puzzle title and a one-line fun fact on every page (this is where the "educational" angle comes in)
- **Difficulty:** early puzzles have words going right and down only. Diagonals come next, then backwards words.

### Sudoku
- **Price:** $9.99–11.99
- **Large print:** 1 puzzle per page, grid about 6.5 in square, digits 24pt+
- **Standard:** 2 puzzles per page, digits 18pt+
- **Difficulty:** Easy → Medium → Hard → Expert, graded by solving technique
- **Branding idea:** "Stay Sharp Sudoku" style (see Section 6 for the trademark warning)

### Crossword
- The hardest puzzle type to do well, because **every clue has to be written and checked**.
- Themed "criss-cross" puzzles (free-form grids of theme words) are realistic to start with. Full newspaper-style crosswords are a later project.
- Clues must be accurate and readable: no obscure words, and nothing only a crossword expert would know.

### Journals
- **Price:** $7.99–9.99
- **Size:** 6 × 9 in or 5.5 × 8.5 in, 120–150 pages, cream paper
- **Page styles:** half blank / half lined, fully blank, fully lined (wide ruled, about 11/32 in / 8.7 mm between lines)
- **Themes:** cat, kawaii, pastel
- **Candid note:** blank and lined journals are the **most crowded category on KDP**, and many sell nothing. A journal only stands out if it offers something extra, such as prompts, a themed page design, or a matching set with one of our puzzle books. Treat journals as a secondary line, not the main business.

## 4. Design and layout

**Fonts**
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible** (free, designed for low-vision readers) for grids, word lists, and digits.
- One simple heading font. Never more than two fonts in a book.

**Colors**
- Interior: black and white only, high contrast, no gray letters, no clip art.
- Covers: a **neutral base color** (cream, sage, warm gray, navy) with **one strong accent color** for the title. Fully neutral covers disappear at thumbnail size on Amazon. Check every cover at thumbnail size next to the top 10 competitors.

**Using the book comfortably**
- KDP paperbacks are glue-bound and do not lie flat, so the design has to account for it:
  - Use an inside margin **well above the KDP minimum**. Aim for **0.75–0.875 in** on puzzle books.
  - KDP minimum inside margins *(verify)*: 24–150 pages: 0.375 in · 151–300: 0.5 in · 301–500: 0.625 in
  - Outside, top, and bottom margins at least 0.5 in
  - Keep grids centered and away from the spine edge
- Books with at least **79 pages** can have text on the spine. Always use spine text so the book is readable on a shelf.

**Page structure (puzzle books)**
1. Title page
2. How to Play (one page per puzzle type)
3. Puzzles, getting harder through the book
4. Blank note pages between sections (useful for scratch work)
5. Answer key in the back, 4 solutions per page, labeled with puzzle number and page
6. Last page: "More books in this series" and a free printable offer (see Section 8)

## 5. Pricing and royalties

KDP paperback royalty = royalty rate × list price − printing cost *(verify)*

- **Royalty rate:** 60% at $9.99 and above, **50% below $9.99** on Amazon.com *(verify)*. **Price most books at $9.99 or more.**
- **Black and white printing, 110+ pages:** about $1.00 + $0.012 per page *(verify)*

| Book | List price | Pages | Est. print cost | Est. royalty per sale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3-in-1 | $12.99 | 200 | ~$3.40 | **~$4.40** |
| Word search | $10.99 | 120 | ~$2.45 | **~$4.15** |
| Sudoku | $9.99 | 120 | ~$2.45 | **~$3.55** |
| Journal | $8.99 | 120 | ~$2.45 | **~$2.05** |

Journals earn about half as much per sale as puzzle books, which is one more reason to keep them secondary.

## 6. Brand

- Choose **one brand name** to use across every book as the author/publisher name and in a KDP series.
- **Ideas:** Brain Buzz, Stay Sharp, ...
- **Trademark warning:** "Staying Sharp" is an AARP brain health program, so "Stay Sharp" is risky. Before choosing a name, search it in the **USPTO trademark database** (tmsearch.uspto.gov) and on Amazon Books.
- Make each series look consistent: the same cover layout, title position, and accent color system. A buyer should recognize our books at a glance.

## 7. Themes and timing

### Evergreen, educational themes (sell all year)
America · cats · plants · months · sea creatures · countries · holidays · the alphabet

Each puzzle includes a short fact related to its theme, which is what makes the book "educational" and not just a word list.

### Seasonal books
- **Publish at least 2 months before the holiday.** Amazon needs about 6 weeks to index a new book and start showing it in search. Christmas is the exception: publish by **late August / early September**.
- **Stick to broad themes** ("Christmas," "Cozy Winter") and avoid niche themes (a single TV show, a single hobby).
- **Never use trademarked names** (characters, brands, sports teams) in titles or word lists.

| Occasion | Date | Publish by |
|---|---|---|
| Christmas 2026 | Dec 25 | ASAP. Already late; treat it as a test run |
| Winter / New Year | Jan 1 | Nov 1, 2026 |
| Valentine's Day | Feb 14, 2027 | Dec 14, 2026 |
| Easter / Spring | Mar 28, 2027 | Jan 28, 2027 |
| Mother's Day | May 9, 2027 | Mar 9, 2027 |
| Father's Day | Jun 20, 2027 | Apr 20, 2027 |
| Grandparents Day | Sep 12, 2027 | Jul 12, 2027 |
| Halloween / Autumn | Oct 31, 2027 | Aug 31, 2027 |
| Christmas 2027 | Dec 25, 2027 | **Early Sep 2027** |

## 8. Listing and marketing

**Wording that sells** (use it naturally, not stuffed in):
large print · for adults · brain workout / brain games · stay sharp · gift for seniors / grandma / mom

**"Train your brain!"** Advertise puzzles as a fun, healthy mental workout. Do **not** make medical claims such as "prevents dementia," "clinically proven," or "improves memory by X%." Amazon can remove listings for health claims, and they aren't honest.

**Every listing includes:**
- A title and subtitle in plain language, e.g. *"Large Print Word Search for Adults: 100 Cozy Christmas Puzzles with Solutions"*
- All 7 backend keyword slots filled with phrases not already in the title
- 3 categories chosen carefully (less crowded categories make a "#1 New Release" badge easier to earn)
- A+ Content showing a real interior page

**Free printable:** the last page offers a free printable puzzle pack in exchange for an email address. Email the list whenever a new book launches.

## 9. Competitor research checklist

For each category we plan to enter, study the **top 10 best sellers** and record:

| Record | Why |
|---|---|
| Trim size and page count | Shows which formats buyers choose |
| Price | Sets our price range |
| Puzzles per page, grid size, font size | Shows the standard we need to beat |
| Cover style (colors, fonts, imagery) | What stands out at thumbnail size |
| Number of reviews and star rating | Shows whether the leaders are beatable |
| **Complaints in 1–3 star reviews** | The fastest way to find what buyers want fixed |
| Best Sellers Rank (BSR) | Rough sales estimate; track over time with Keepa |

---

## 10. Rules we don't break

- **No fake reviews.** Don't ask friends or family to review, and don't use review or "launch" services. It can get the KDP account banned.
- **Disclose AI use to KDP** when uploading. Word lists, clues, or facts drafted with AI count, even after editing.
- **No trademarks** in titles, word lists, or covers.
- **No copycat books.** Each book has its own puzzles and word lists.
